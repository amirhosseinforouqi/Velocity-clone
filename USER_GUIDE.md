# User Guide

## Create a lead

1. Click **New Lead** at the top of the left menu.
2. Enter the name, email and phone. If the email or phone already exists you'll see the match first.
3. Choose what the application is for and the employment situation. These pick the starting document checklist.
4. Click **Create Lead Only**, or **Create Lead & Invite Client** to review and confirm the welcome email.

The file opens straight away.

## Work in a file

- The top shows the client, stage, portal status, ratios, dates and document counts.
- Use the tabs in order: Client & Applicants → Employment & Income → Assets & Liabilities → Property & Mortgage.
- Type in any field and press **Save** (it saves every tab at once).
- **Add an employment / other income / asset / liability** adds another entry. **Remove** asks first and can be
  undone until you save.
- To change the client's name, email or phone, click **Edit Client**.

## Documents

- The **Documents** tab lists what was requested. **Request Documents** adds one item or a whole checklist.
- **Cancel** removes a request (uploaded versions stay in the audit trail).
- When the client uploads, the item shows "Uploaded". Choose **Accept** or **Request replacement**.
- **Upload for client** lets you add a file yourself: click it or drop a file on it. PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to 10 MB.

## Invite the client to the portal

Click **Invite Client**, read the email, and confirm. The client gets a one-time link, valid for 7 days, and
chooses their own password. If email isn't set up, the screen says so and shows the link once so you can pass it
on yourself. Use **Send password reset link** on the Messages tab if they lose access.

## Messages and notes

**Send Message** opens the Messages tab. Secure messages go to the client's portal; the notification email never
includes the message text. Staff notes are internal.

## Move the file along

**Change Stage** offers only the allowed next steps. For a lead, **Set lead stage…** tracks follow-up. Moving a
lead to Pre-Approvals or New Deals converts it; nothing is retyped. The Pipeline updates immediately.

## Ratios and calculators

GDS, TDS and LTV appear once income, price, down payment (or loan amount), rate and amortization are in. They
use general defaults and are a guide only — check each lender's own rules. An administrator can change the
stress-test buffer, rate floor and revolving-debt percentage in Admin → Organization → Qualifying Defaults. **Calculators** in the top bar start
from the open file's figures and never change the file.

## Totals, calendar and first contact

Total Assets, Total Liabilities and Net Worth add themselves up from the entries. A new lead creates a "First
contact" task for today. Closing, conditions and follow-up dates from each file appear on the Calendar; click one
to open the file.

## SIN

Enter 9 digits and save. After that only the last three digits are shown. To change it, type a new number.

## Lender and conditions

Lender conditions are on the Documents tab (add from the library or add your own; click or drop a file to attach it). The **Lender & Stage** tab
holds progress steps, what the client sees, close-out and aftercare. Sending to a lender electronically is not
available; record submissions manually.
